## 1. Document Purpose & Phase Objective

This document represents the authoritative deliverable for **SA-01.3: Category Mapping**.

Building directly upon the frozen **SA-01.1 Master Award Inventory** and **SA-01.2 Award Criterion Matrix**, this phase establishes the exact portfolio category boundaries that each award criterion and scoring subsection is permitted to consume.

### Non-Negotiable Architectural Principles
1. **Category Match $\neq$ Award Points:** A portfolio record belonging to an authorized category is merely a candidate input. Points are never assigned based solely on category membership. Downstream evaluation of Sub-Category (SA-01.4), Metadata Predicates (SA-01.5), and Scoring Rules (SA-01.6) is strictly required.
2. **Deterministic Boundaries:** Unsupported category routes are strictly rejected.
3. **Multi-Category & Exclusivity Documentation:** Criteria consuming multiple related categories (e.g., Church Ministry + Community Service) and criteria requiring strict category exclusivity (e.g., Sports, Socio-Cultural, Journalism) are explicitly demarcated.
4. **Preservation of Frozen Baselines:** No award names, criterion identifiers, maximum points, or accumulation behaviors from SA-01.1 and SA-01.2 have been altered.
