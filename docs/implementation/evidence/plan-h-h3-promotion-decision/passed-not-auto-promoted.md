# Plan H Phase H3 — Passed Evaluation Does Not Auto-Promote

### Governance Rule
> **A Passed evaluation may proceed to deliberation, but Passed does not guarantee promotion. HR records the final Promotion Decision. Only an explicitly approved promotion may update rank/title.**

### Verification Proof
- `Passed` evaluations remain at their current evaluated rank until an explicit `Approved` promotion decision is recorded.
- Without an explicit promotion decision, `promotion_decision` remains `null` and `rank_change_applied` remains `false`.
