## 1. Document Purpose & Phase Objective

This document represents the authoritative deliverable for **SA-01.4: Sub-Category Mapping**.

Building directly upon the frozen baselines of **SA-01.1**, **SA-01.2**, and **SA-01.3**, this phase decomposes each authorized criterion/category route into its exact allowed child sub-categories (`portfolio_subcategories`).

### Non-Negotiable Architectural Principles
1. **Zero Sub-Category Inheritance:** An authorized parent category does **not** grant blanket validity to all child sub-categories. Every single child sub-category is explicitly classified as either `ALLOWED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
2. **Sub-Category Match $\neq$ Award Points:** Qualifying under an allowed sub-category validates the structural route only. It does not award points until SA-01.5 (Metadata & Conditions), SA-01.6 (Scoring Rules), and SA-01.7 (Validation Cases) are satisfied.
3. **Paired-Award Consistency:** Gender-paired awards (`Male` / `Female`) and lifecycle-paired awards (`Graduating` / `Non-Graduating`) have their sub-category routes synchronized and audited for structural symmetry.
4. **Preservation of Prior Frozen States:** All award identifiers, criterion codes, maximum scores, point caps, and parent category boundaries remain 100% frozen.
