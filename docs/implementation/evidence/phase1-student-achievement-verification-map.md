# AchieveNest — Plan 04 Phase 1
## Evidence: Verification Workflow & Status Map

---

### 1. Verification Status Matrix

| Status | Meaning | Student Can Edit? | Eligible for Verification? | Eligible for Award Scoring? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `draft` | Unsubmitted record saved by student | YES | NO | NO |
| `submitted` | Submitted by student, awaiting review | NO (Read-only) | YES | NO |
| `under_review` | Claimed or currently being reviewed by verifier | NO | YES | NO |
| `revisions_requested` | Returned to student with reviewer remarks | YES | NO (Until resubmitted) | NO |
| `verified` | Formally approved and verified by authorized personnel | NO (Locked) | NO (Already complete) | **YES** |
| `rejected` | Denied / ineligible record | NO | NO | NO |

---

### 2. Verification Authorities

- **Program Coordinator**: Primary authority for co-curricular and academic achievements of students within their assigned academic programs.
- **Organization Moderator**: Authorized for university student organizations and club activities.
- **Dean / Assistant Dean**: College-level oversight.
- **OSAD Administrator / Staff**: Central administrative authority.
