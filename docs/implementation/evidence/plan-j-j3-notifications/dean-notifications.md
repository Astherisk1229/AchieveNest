# Phase J3 Evidence: Dean & HR Reviewer Notification Workflows

## Dean Reviewer Notifications
- Notified on `portfolio_submitted` for assigned college faculty.
- Notified on `portfolio_resubmitted` when candidate uploads a revised version.

## HR Reviewer Notifications
- Notified on `portfolio_submitted` for non-academic personnel and administrator portfolios.
- Notified on `reviewer_assigned` when an evaluation is routed to HR.
