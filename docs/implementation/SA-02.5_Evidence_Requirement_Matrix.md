## 1. Document Purpose & Core Invariants

This document defines the definitive **Supporting Evidence Requirements Matrix** across all active achievement sub-categories in AchieveNest.

### Non-Negotiable Invariants:
1. **Evidence Does Not Verify Itself:** Uploading evidence moves the record to `submitted` / `pending_verification`; only an authorized Evaluator review confirms `verified` status.
2. **Standard Accepted Formats:** `PDF`, `DOC`, `DOCX`, `PNG`, `JPG`, `JPEG` (Max 10 MiB per file).
3. **Multi-File Allowance:** Students may upload up to 5 supporting documents per achievement record.
4. **Resubmission on Edit:** Replacing or modifying evidence on a verified record resets the status to `submitted`.
