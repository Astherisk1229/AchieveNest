## HR Office Reviewer Scope Audit

### 1. Confirmed In-Scope Routing
The HR Office Reviewer Queue (`hr_staff` / `hr_admin`) includes strictly the confirmed HR-routed classifications:
1. `Non-Teaching Faculty + Non-Academic`
2. `College Deans`
3. `Vice President for Academics`
4. `Vice President for Administration`

### 2. Academic Faculty Exclusion from Direct HR Queue
- Academic Faculty evaluations routed to College Deans are **excluded** from the active direct HR Reviewer Queue.
- General HR administrative directory visibility does not grant direct reviewer assignment or rating authority over departmental faculty where a Dean is the designated evaluator.
