## AchieveNest — Responsive Sidebar Navigation State & Route Transition Stability

# 1. Active State & Role Synchronization

### 1.1 Router Authority
- Active route highlighting is computed purely from `location.pathname` and `searchParams.get('tab')` via `isTabOrPathActive(item)`.
- Reclicking the active route causes zero state resets.

### 1.2 Role-Based Navigation Generation
- Permitted navigation links are generated by `getAuthorizedNavigationForSession(userSession)` in `src/config/personnelRoleNavigation.js`.
- Switching roles (e.g. from `program_coordinator` to `dean`) recomputes authorized navigation immediately.
- `Stale role nav items after switch`: **0**
- `Manual browser refreshes after role switch`: **0**
