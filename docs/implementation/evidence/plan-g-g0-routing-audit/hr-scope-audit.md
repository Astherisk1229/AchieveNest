# Personnel Evaluation Track — Plan G — Phase G0: HR Scope Audit

## HR Office Evaluator Authority & Oversight Scope

### 1. Direct Evaluation Responsibilities
The HR Office (`hr_staff` / `hr_admin`) acts as the direct, authoritative evaluator for:
- Non-Teaching Faculty + Non-Academic Personnel (including Area A official rating sheet input);
- College Deans (who cannot be evaluated by themselves or peer deans);
- Vice President for Academics;
- Vice President for Administration.

### 2. Oversight vs Direct Scoring Separation
- **Direct Evaluator Route**: HR records official accepted points and ratings for evaluations directly assigned to the HR queue.
- **Dean Evaluation Oversight**: For evaluations assigned to College Deans, HR possesses oversight visibility across the university, but does not overwrite or replace an assigned Dean's official evaluation determinations without formal institutional reassignment.
