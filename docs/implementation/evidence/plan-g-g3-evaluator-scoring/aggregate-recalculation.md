# Aggregate Recalculation & Area Cap Verification

## Authoritative Total Recalculation

Upon saving evaluator accepted scores, totals are dynamically recalculated respecting Plan F area caps:

### Administrators Scale Cap Enforcement:
- **Area A**: Max `70.0` points
- **Area B**: Max `50.0` points (e.g. raw sum `60.0` points is capped to `50.0`)
- **Area C**: Max `40.0` points
- **Overall Total**: Capped at `160.0` points

### Non-Teaching Scale Cap Enforcement:
- **Area A**: Max `90.0` points (Job Performance 50 + Personal Attitudes 10 + Efficiency 30)
- **Area B**: Max `60.0` points
- **Overall Total**: Capped at `150.0` points
