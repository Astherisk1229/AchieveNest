# Evidence Requirements — Administrators Scale

## Evidence Policy
Under Plan F Phase F2 and frozen Phase F0 rules, all accomplishment-submittable criteria on the Administrators Ranking Scale require verifiable documentary evidence (`evidence_required = true`).

## Criterion Breakdown

| Criterion Code | Criterion Display Label | Evidence Required? | Accepted Proof Types |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A1_DEGREES` | Degree/s | **YES** | Official Diploma, Official Transcript of Records (TOR), Certificate of Units Earned |
| `A2_PROFESSIONAL_ORGANIZATION_MEMBERSHIP` | Active Membership to Professional Organizations | **YES** | Certificate of Membership, Appointment Letter / Board Resolution (for Officers) |
| `A3_SEMINARS_TRAININGS` | Attendance to Seminars/Trainings | **YES** | Certificate of Attendance / Participation |
| `B1_GUEST_LECTURER_CONSULTANT_JUDGE_RESOURCE_PERSON` | Guest Lecturer / Consultant / Judge / Resource Person | **YES** | Certificate of Appreciation / Recognition, Invitation Letter, Program Copy |
| `B2_PUBLICATION` | Publication | **YES** | Published Copy, ISSN/ISBN Verification, Copyright Page, Acceptance Letter |
| `B3_CONDUCT_OF_RESEARCH` | Conduct of Research | **YES** | Completed Research Report, Institutional Approval, Research Abstract |
| `B4_PROFESSIONAL_RECOGNITION_AWARDS` | Professional Recognition or Awards | **YES** | Plaque/Certificate of Award, Official Citation, Nomination Endorsement |
| `B5_INSTRUCTIONAL_MATERIALS` | Production of Instructional Materials | **YES** | Copy of Bound Material, Departmental Evaluation / Adoption Certification |
| `B6_CREATIVE_WORK` | Creative Work | **YES** | Exhibit Catalog, Copyright/Patent Certificate, Evidence of Presentation |
| `C1_EXTRA_CURRICULAR_ORGANIZATIONS` | Involvement in Extra-Curricular Activities | **YES** | Appointment Letter as Moderator/Coach, Committee Memo, Service Certificate |
| `C2_COMMUNITY_INVOLVEMENT` | Community Involvement | **YES** | Certificate of Community Service, Church Endorsement, Outreach Documentation |
| `C3_YEARS_OF_SERVICE` | Years of Service at NDMU | *N/A* | Verified Employment Record / HR Master Record (Server-derived) |

## Storage & Ingestion Boundary
Evidence uploads leverage the existing Plan A / Plan I evidence pipeline. No redundant file tables or separate binary stores are created.
