# Certificate Studio surface brief

Scope: `/osad/certificate-templates/:familyId/versions/:versionId/edit`
Mode: Operate

Audience: OSAD staff authoring governed student certificate templates.
Job: shape an institutional certificate, verify readiness, save a draft, and publish an immutable version.
Constraints: current backend registries and lifecycle remain authoritative; synthetic preview only; Plan 2 asset storage/rendering fidelity is explicitly deferred.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A restrained institutional document studio where the certificate—not app chrome—dominates. It refuses the current form-heavy modal and keeps technical schemas invisible.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool-slate work surfaces, emerald as the sole action/selection accent, compact sans-serif controls, hairline panel boundaries, square document geometry, and a white print canvas with formal NDMU character.

STORY: OSAD enters a draft, chooses a design section, makes controlled visual or content decisions, sees the synthetic certificate update immediately, resolves readiness issues, then validates and publishes through the governed backend.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sticky workflow bar spans the top. Below it, a narrow section rail, a focused settings column, and a preview-dominant document stage form the desktop workspace. On tablet the rail becomes horizontal; on mobile Design and Preview become explicit panes with sticky actions.

FORM: Brief-pinned three-part document studio, selected directly from Plan 1; concept seed not applicable because layout, visual direction, controls, and interaction hierarchy are specified.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
