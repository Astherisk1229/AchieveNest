# Evidence Requirements — Non-Teaching Scale

## Evidence Policy
Under Plan F Phase F3 and frozen Phase F0 rules, all accomplishment-submittable criteria on the Non-Teaching Personnel Ranking Scale require verifiable documentary evidence (`evidence_required = true`).

## Criterion Breakdown

| Criterion Code | Criterion Display Label | Evidence Required? | Accepted Proof Types |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A.1` | Job Performance | *N/A* | Evaluation-Only (Supervisor Assessment) |
| `A.2` | Personal Attitudes and Qualities | *N/A* | Evaluation-Only (Supervisor Assessment) |
| `A.3` | Efficiency | *N/A* | Evaluation-Only (Supervisor Assessment) |
| `B.1` | Involvement in School Activities | **YES** | Official Designation Letter / Special Order / Appointment Letter |
| `B.2` | Community Involvement | **YES** | Certificate of Service / Appreciation from Parish/LGU/NGO |
| `B.3` | Number of Years at NDMU | *N/A* | Verified Employment Record / HR Master Record (Server-derived) |
| `B.4` | Invited as Judge, Lecturer, Resource Person | **YES** | Invitation Letter & Certificate of Appreciation |
| `B.5` | Recognition / Meritorious Award | **YES** | Award Plaque Photo / Official Certificate of Award |

## Storage & Pipeline Boundary
Document uploads leverage the existing Plan A / Plan I evidence pipeline. No duplicate tables or binary stores are created.
