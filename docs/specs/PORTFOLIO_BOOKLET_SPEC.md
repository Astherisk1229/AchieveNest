# NDMU FACULTY PORTFOLIO BOOKLET & CATEGORY SPECIFICATION (`PORTFOLIO_BOOKLET_SPEC.md`)

## Executive Summary
The **NDMU Faculty Portfolio Presentation Booklet** ([`PersonnelPortfolioCanvaView.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/Admin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/achievenest/src/components/personnel/PersonnelPortfolioCanvaView.jsx)) is a 16-slide interactive, Canva-style presentation deck generated directly from a personnel member's verified portfolio draft.

## 2. Detailed Slide-by-Slide Content Specifications

### Page 01: Cover & Title Deck
- **Header**: Official Notre Dame of Marbel University (NDMU) Seal & Brand Colors (`#1b4332` Dark Forest Emerald & Gold `#f59e0b`).
- **Title**: *Official Faculty Academic Ranking & Promotion Dossier*
- **Fields**:
  - `personnel_name`: Full Name & Academic Rank (e.g. *Dr. Maria Santos, Associate Professor*)
  - `department_name`: College / Department (e.g. *College of Information Technology*)
  - `employee_id`: NDMU Employee ID
  - `academic_year`: Current Academic Evaluation Cycle (e.g. *AY 2025-2026*)

### Page 02: Table of Contents & Navigational Index
- **Interactive Section Index**:
  1. Executive Summary & Scoreboard (Page 3)
  2. Faculty Profile & Service Background (Page 4)
  3. Area A: Professional Development (Pages 5 – 8)
  4. Area B: Productivity & Creative Work (Pages 9 – 14)
  5. Area C: Service & Leadership (Page 15)
  6. Administrative Authentication & Sign-Off (Page 16)
- **Navigation Controls**: 1-Click quick jump buttons to any section in the presentation deck.

### Page 03: Executive Summary & Scoreboard Ledger
- **Overview Cards**:
  - **Area A (Professional Development)**: Claimed/Accepted Points vs **70 Max Cap**
  - **Area B (Productivity & Creative Work)**: Claimed/Accepted Points vs **50 Max Section Cap**
  - **Area C (Service & Leadership)**: Claimed/Accepted Points vs **40 Max Cap**
  - **Grand Capped Score**: Total Capped Points vs **160 Max Total**
- **Status Tag**: `DRAFT` | `SUBMITTED_TO_DEP_SEC` | `ENDORSED_TO_HR` | `HR_APPROVED`

### Page 04: Faculty Profile & Service History
- Biography summary, educational background, years of service at NDMU, and primary teaching/research domains.

### Page 05 – 08: Area A — Professional Development Slides
- **Page 05**: Area A Scoreboard & Summary (70 Pts Ceiling).
- **Page 06 (A.1 Degrees)**: List of earned Ph.D./Master's degrees, units earned, granting institutions, and attached diploma PDF proofs.
- **Page 07 (A.2 Memberships)**: List of active professional organization memberships (Officer vs Regular Member).
- **Page 08 (A.3 Seminars & Trainings)**: Detailed list of attended seminars categorized by scale (Local, Regional, National, International) with certificates of completion.

### Page 09 – 14: Area B — Productivity & Creative Work Slides
- **Page 09**: Area B Scoreboard & Summary (50 Pts Section Ceiling).
- **Page 10 (B.1 Lectures & Speaking)**: Speaking engagements, keynote addresses, consultancies, and judging roles.
- **Page 11 (B.2 Publications)**: Journal articles, Scopus indexed papers, research outputs, monographs, compilations, and published books.
- **Page 12 (B.3 Research)**: Principal investigator roles, research grants, completed institutional or funded research projects.
- **Page 13 (B.4 Awards & Honors)**: Professional awards, citations, and recognitions (Nominee vs Awardee).
- **Page 14 (B.5 & B.6 Instructional & Creative)**: Modules, audio-visual aids, reviewers, workbooks, and creative artistic output.

### Page 15: Area C — Service & Leadership Slide
- **Page 15 (C.1, C.2 & C.3 Service & Longevity)**:
  - Extra-curricular club moderation, coaching, and committee service (Up to 20 pts per role).
  - Community, civic, and church involvement (Up to 25 pts).
  - NDMU Service Tenure points (1 pt per 2 years up to 10 pts max).

### Page 16: Authentication & Administrative Endorsement Sign-off
- Formal authentication section containing:
  - Faculty Member Signature & Submission Date
  - Department Secretary Verification & Endorsement Signature
  - HR Evaluation Officer Approval & Official Seal
