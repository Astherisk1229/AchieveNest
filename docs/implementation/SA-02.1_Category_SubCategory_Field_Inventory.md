## 1. Document Purpose & Phase Scope

This document establishes the comprehensive **Student-Facing Submission Field Inventory** across all active portfolio categories and sub-categories in AchieveNest.

### Non-Negotiable Invariants:
1. **No Field Invention:** Every listed field is backed by the authoritative database schema, backend metadata validator, or frozen SA-01.5 conditions.
2. **Sub-Category Scoped:** Fields are assigned strictly to relevant sub-categories to prevent generic form leakage.
3. **Requirement Status Scope:** In accordance with SA-02.1 rules, specific requirement levels are marked `TO_BE_FINALIZED_IN_SA-02.2` (except structural record essentials).
4. **Internal Field Protection:** Workflow internals (`verification_status`, `verified_by`, `scoring_status`, internal UUIDs) are omitted from student inputs.
