# OFFICIAL NDMU RATING SHEET FOR RANKING SPECIFICATION (`NDMU_RATING_SHEET_FOR_RANKING_SPEC.md`)

## 📄 Executive Summary & Document Overview
- **Institution**: Notre Dame of Marbel University (NDMU)
- **Document Title**: Official Rating Sheet for Ranking
- **Total Document Scope**: 5 Pages Specification Schedule
- **Purpose**: An official evaluation rubric used by NDMU for faculty and staff ranking, promotion, tenure, and institutional assessment. It measures performance across three main pillars:
  - **A. Professional Development**
  - **B. Productivity and Creative Work**
  - **C. Service and Leadership**

---

## 📊 Detailed Structure & Scoring System

| Evaluation Area Category | Section Description | Maximum Point Cap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Area A** | **Professional Development** | **70 Points Max** |
| **Area B** | **Productivity & Creative Work** | **50 Points Max (Section Cap)** |
| **Area C** | **Service & Leadership** | **40 Points Max** |
| **GRAND TOTAL** | **Capped Evaluation Total** | **160 Points Max** |

---

## 🅰️ Section A: Professional Development (Maximum: 70 Points)
This section evaluates educational qualifications, professional affiliations, and continuous learning.

### A.1 Degree/s (Max 40 Points)
- **Ph.D. Degree Holder**: 40 pts max
- **Ph.D. Units Earned**: 2 pts per 3 units (Max 10 pts — e.g., 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 pts)
- **Master's (MA/MS) Degree Holder**: 20 pts max
- **Master's (MA/MS) Units Earned**: 1 pt per 3 units (Max 10 pts — scale: 1 to 10 pts)

### A.2 Active Membership in Professional Organizations (Max 10 Points)
- **Officer**: 10 pts per office or position held
- **Member**: 5 pts per active membership

### A.3 Attendance to Seminars / Trainings for Professional Development (Max 20 Points)
Points are allocated per seminar based on geographic and organizational scale:
- **In-House / Local Institutional**: 3 pts
- **City / Provincial**: 4 pts
- **Regional**: 6 pts
- **National**: 8 pts
- **International**: 10 pts

---

## 🅱️ Section B: Productivity and Creative Work (Maximum Section Cap: 50 Points)
This section awards points for scholarly output, public lectures, published works, research, and instructional materials.

### B.1 Guest Lecturer / Consultant / Judge / Resource Person (Max 40 Points)
Points depend on:
- **Sponsoring Body**: NDMU vs. External Agencies / Other Schools
- **Duration / Extent of Talk**: 1 hr, Half day, 1 day, 2 days, or More days
- **Audience Scale**: Local, Regional, National, International
- **Role**: Reactor, Keynote/Facilitator, Consultant/Speaker, Judge/Organizer
- **Point Range**: 1 to 10 points per event based on complexity and scope.

### B.2 Publication (Scholarly Paper / Article / Research Output / Book) (Max 40 Points)
Points are graded by type of publication and geographical scope:
- **Commentary / Reviews**: 1 to 5 pts
- **Compilation**: 1 to 5 pts
- **Article**: 2 to 5 pts
- **Scholarly Paper**: 3 to 8 pts
- **Monograph Output**: 4 to 8 pts
- **Research Output**: 3 to 10 pts
- **Book**: 5 to 10 pts
- **Scope Weighting**: Local, Regional, National, International.

### B.3 Conduct of Research (Max 40 Points)
Direct recognition for leading and completing research projects.

### B.4 Professional Recognition or Awards (Max 40 Points)
- **Nominee**:
  - Local: 5 pts
  - Regional / Provincial: 15 pts
  - National: 20 pts
  - International: 20 pts
- **Awardee**:
  - Local: 10 pts
  - Regional / Provincial: 30 pts
  - National: 40 pts
  - International: 40 pts

### B.5 Production of Instructional Materials (Max 40 Points)
Recognizes faculty creation of teaching aids and materials:
- **Audio-Visual Aids**: 10 pts
- **Modules**: 10 pts (Local) / 30 pts (Regional) / 40 pts (National / International)
- **Bound Reviewers**: 10 to 20 pts
- **Others (Bound Workbooks, Exercises, Lecture Notes)**: 20 pts

### B.6 Creative Work (Max 20 Points)
For artistic, creative, or specialized non-traditional academic creations.

---

## ☪️ Section C: Service and Leadership (Maximum: 40 Points)
This section measures institutional commitment, extracurricular involvement, community service, and longevity.

### C.1 Involvement in Extra-Curricular Activities / Recognized School Orgs (Max 30 Points)
- **C.1.1 Moderator of Clubs / Organizations**: Up to 20 pts
- **C.1.2 Coach / Trainer**: Up to 20 pts
- **C.1.3 Membership in Working Committees**: Up to 20 pts
- **C.1.4 Service during Intramurals / School Events**: Up to 20 pts

### C.2 Community Involvement (Max 30 Points)
- **C.2.1 Active Involvement in Church Activities**: Up to 25 pts
- **C.2.2 Active Involvement in Community / Civic Activities**: Up to 25 pts
- **C.2.3 Support to Charity and Community Projects**: Up to 5 pts

### C.3 Years of Service at NDMU (Max 10 Points)
- **Rule**: 1 point for every 2 years of service at NDMU.
- **Scale**: 1 to 20 years of service (Yields 1 to 10 Points).

## 🔑 Key Takeaways
- **Balanced Academic Matrix**: Prioritizes advanced degrees (Ph.D. carrying 40 pts), continuous scholarly publishing, and research.
- **Tiered Recognition**: Strictly scales point awards based on level of reach (Local → Provincial → Regional → National → International).
- **Institutional Loyalty & Values**: Directly rewards service to NDMU (years of service, club moderation) alongside community and church involvement.
