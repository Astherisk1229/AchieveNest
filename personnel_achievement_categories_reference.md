**Document Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Executive Overview & Scoring Structure

This reference guide details the official categorization hierarchy, sub-categories, point schedules, scope multipliers, area ceiling caps, and mandatory verification proof requirements for **NDMU University Personnel Ranking Portfolios**.

### University Area Maximum Ceiling Summary

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               NDMU RANKING AREA CEILING SUMMARY                                    |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Area Name                              | Area Max Ceiling  | Primary Components & Sub-Categories   |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------+---------------------------------------+
| Area A: Professional Development       | 70 Points Max     | Degrees, Units, Prof. Memberships,    |
|                                        |                   | Seminars & Trainings                  |
| Area B: Productivity & Creative Work   | 50 Points Max     | Guest Lectures, Publications,         |
|                                        |                   | Conduct of Research, Awards,          |
|                                        |                   | Instructional Mat., Creative Work     |
| Area C: Service & Leadership           | 40 Points Max     | School Activities, Clubs, Community,  |
|                                        |                   | Church Service, NDMU Service Years    |
+----------------------------------------+-------------------+---------------------------------------+
| OVERALL MAXIMUM RANKING SCORE          | 160 Points Max                                            |
+----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------+
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict HR Area Ceiling Capping Rule:**  
> Even if a personnel member earns total points exceeding an area ceiling (e.g., 65 points in Area B), **ONLY the maximum allowed points for that area are accepted** towards their official ranking score.

## 2. Area A: Professional Development (Maximum Ceiling: 70 Points)

### A.1 Degree/s & Graduate Units (Sub-Ceiling: 40 Points Max)

| Sub-Category / Option | Point Schedule / Formula | Sub-Ceiling | Required Verification Proof Document |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ph.D. Degree Holder** | `40 Points` (Flat) | 40 Pts | Official Diploma / Transcript of Records (TOR) |
| **Ph.D. Units Earned** | `2 Points per 3 units` | 10 Pts Max | Official Transcript of Records (TOR) / Units Certificate |
| **MA Degree Holder** | `20 Points` (Flat) | 20 Pts | Official Diploma / Transcript of Records (TOR) |
| **MA Units Earned** | `1 Point per 3 units` | 10 Pts Max | Official Transcript of Records (TOR) / Units Certificate |

#### Units Calculation Scale:
- **Ph.D. Units:** 3 units = 2 pts | 6 units = 4 pts | 9 units = 6 pts | 12 units = 8 pts | 15+ units = 10 pts
- **MA Units:** 3 units = 1 pt | 6 units = 2 pts | 9 units = 3 pts | 12 units = 4 pts ... | 30+ units = 10 pts

### C.3 NDMU Years of Service Credit (Sub-Ceiling: 10 Points Max)

| Sub-Category / Type | Formula Rule | Sub-Ceiling | Required Verification Proof Document |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NDMU Service Credit** | `1 Point for every 2 full years` | 10 Pts Max | HR Service Record / Certificate of Employment |

$$\text{Service Points} = \min\left(10, \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Years of Service}}{2} \right\rfloor\right)$$

*(Scale: 2 yrs = 1 pt | 4 yrs = 2 pts | 6 yrs = 3 pts | 10 yrs = 5 pts | 20+ yrs = 10 pts)*
