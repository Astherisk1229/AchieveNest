## 1. Audit Objective

The objective of **Phase SA-01.7A** is to inspect and document every authoritative source of scoring rules across the AchieveNest system. This audit verifies where award point values, counting rules, caps, and evaluation logic originate in the codebase and official documentation, ensuring zero reliance on undocumented or ad-hoc constants.

## 3. Award-by-Award Scoring Source Analysis

### 3.1 Notre Dame Award (`NOTRE_DAME_AWARD`)
- **Scholastic (30.00 pts):** Evaluator scored from transcript (`MANUAL`).
- **Leadership (20.00 pts):** Official handbook dictates 10.00 max for position involvement (`HIGHEST_ONLY`: Univ 10.0, Coll 8.0, Club 6.0) and 10.00 max for citations/seminars (`ACCUMULATIVE`: Nat 5.0, Reg 4.0, Inst 2.5).
- **Church Involvement (20.00 pts):** System operationalization of official rubric into 2 subsections of 10.00 pts each (Campus/Parish Ministry count-limited to 2 items @ 5.00 pts; Initiated Activities count-limited to 2 items @ 5.00 pts).
- **Citations & Special Awards (10.00 pts):** Official rubric for non-academic citations (`ACCUMULATIVE`: Nat 5.0, Reg 3.5, Inst 2.0 capped at 10.00).
- **Character & Disciplinary Record (20.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).

### 3.2 Saint Marcellin Champagnat Award (`SMC_AWARD`)
- **Scholastic (20.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).
- **Leadership (20.00 pts):** Shared leadership structure with NDA (`COMP_SMC_LEAD_INVOLVE` 10.00 max, `COMP_SMC_LEAD_AWARDS` 10.00 max).
- **Community & Church Involvement (30.00 pts):** Official rubric structured into 2 subsections of 15.00 pts each (`COMP_SMC_COMM_INVOLVE` 3 items @ 5.00 pts; `COMP_SMC_COMM_INITIATED` 3 items @ 5.00 pts).
- **Citations & Special Awards (10.00 pts):** Official rubric for non-academic citations (`ACCUMULATIVE` capped at 10.00).
- **Character (20.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).

### 3.3 Leadership Award (`LEADERSHIP_AWARD`)
- **Scholastic (20.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).
- **Campus Leadership (30.00 pts):** Multi-tier subsection structure:
  - `COMP_LEAD_USG_EXEC`: 15.00 max (`HIGHEST_ONLY`: Pres 15.0, VP/Officer 12.0)
  - `COMP_LEAD_CLUB_OFFICER`: 10.00 max (`HIGHEST_ONLY`: Gov/Pres 10.0, Officer 7.0)
  - `COMP_LEAD_SEMINARS_AWARDS`: 5.00 max (`ACCUMULATIVE`: Nat 2.5, Reg 2.0, Inst 1.0)
- **Community Involvement (20.00 pts):** 2 subsections of 10.00 pts each (`COMP_LEAD_COMM_VOLUNTEER` 4 items @ 2.50 pts; `COMP_LEAD_COMM_HEAD` 2 items @ 5.00 pts).
- **Character (20.00 pts) & Interview (10.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).

### 3.4 Campus Journalism Award (`CAMPUS_JOURNALISM_AWARD`)
- **Character & Conduct (20.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).
- **Quality of Published Articles (60.00 pts):**
  - News items: 5 items @ 2.00 pts (10.00 max)
  - Literary items: 5 items @ 2.00 pts (10.00 max)
  - Feature/Editorial items: 5 items @ 2.00 pts (10.00 max)
  - Press Competitions: Nat 15.0, Reg 10.0, Div 5.0 (30.00 max)
- **Leadership in Publication (10.00 pts):**
  - Editorial Board: EIC 6.0, Assoc/Editor 4.0 (`HIGHEST_ONLY`: 6.00 max)
  - Press Seminars/Workshops: 2 items @ 2.00 pts (4.00 max)
- **Panel Interview (10.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).

### 3.5 Sports Awards (`SPORTS_AWARD_FEMALE` & `SPORTS_AWARD_MALE`)
- **Academic Compliance (15.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).
- **Skills & Attitude (20.00 pts):** Individual skills (10.00 max) + Team skills (10.00 max).
- **Participation in Sports Meets (20.00 pts):** Nat/PRISAA 10.0, Reg 7.0 (`ACCUMULATIVE` capped at 20.00).
- **Awards Received (15.00 pts):** Gold/1st 15.0, Silver/2nd 10.0 (`ACCUMULATIVE` capped at 15.00).
- **Interview (10.00 pts):** Evaluator scored (`MANUAL`).

### 3.6 Socio-Cultural Awards (`SOCIO_CULTURAL_AWARD_FEMALE` & `SOCIO_CULTURAL_AWARD_MALE`)
- **Skills & Performance (20.00 pts):** Solo performance (10.00 max) + Ensemble/Group performance (10.00 max).
- **Participation in Cultural Meets (20.00 pts):** Nat/Festival 10.0, Reg 7.0 (`ACCUMULATIVE` capped at 20.00).
- **Awards Received (15.00 pts):** 1st Place 15.0, 2nd Place 10.0 (`ACCUMULATIVE` capped at 15.00).
- Note: 100% automatic structure (55.00 auto pts = 100% award score).

### 3.7 Annual Awards (`STUDENT_LEADER_OF_THE_YEAR`, `MEMBER_OF_THE_YEAR`, `VOLUNTEER_OF_THE_YEAR`, `ATHLETE_OF_THE_YEAR_*`, `PERFORMER_OF_THE_YEAR_*`)
- Point schedules mirror mother awards but enforce strict Academic Year date-range scoping (`occurrence_date` within current AY).

## 4. Audit Findings & Conclusion

1. **Zero Undocumented Constants:** All scoring parameters reside in version-controlled seeders, models, and migrations.
2. **Deterministic Rules:** Every route possesses an explicit point assignment mechanism (`FIXED_PER_RECORD`, `TIERED_LADDER`, or `MANUAL_EVALUATION`).
3. **Audit Status:** `SAFE` — Complete traceability from student achievement submission to awarded point snapshot.
