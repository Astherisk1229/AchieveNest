## 1. Audit Objective & Scope

Phase **SA-01.7O** inspects the existing codebase to ensure the scoring engine enforces the non-negotiable architectural rules of AchieveNest:
1. No category-only or subcategory-only scoring without full predicate matching;
2. No frontend score calculation or hardcoded frontend point constants;
3. No cross-award helper leakage or shared cap depletion;
4. Strict enforcement of lifecycle verification gates (`status == 'verified'`);
5. Deterministic duplicate suppression.
