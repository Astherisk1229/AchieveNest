# Phase D2-2: Helper Text & Explainability

## Prescribed Helper Text Guidelines
The UI presents clear, explainable helper text underneath the Rank / Title dropdown to provide immediate context without implying formal promotion or binding authority.

### 1. New Personnel Records (Onboarding)
- **Resolved State**:
  `Preferred initial rank based on the selected qualification.`
- **Advisory Badge**:
  `[Recommended: <Rank Label>]`

### 2. Existing Personnel Records (Edit Master Data)
- **Resolved State**:
  `Suggested from qualification. HR may change this if the personnel has an existing official rank.`
- **Advisory Badge**:
  `[Recommended: <Rank Label>]`

### 3. Prohibited Terminology
The helper copy strictly avoids phrases that imply administrative finality or promotion approval, such as:
- ❌ "Promotion approved"
- ❌ "Automatic promotion applied"
- ❌ "Final rank determined"
- ❌ "Rank upgraded by system"
