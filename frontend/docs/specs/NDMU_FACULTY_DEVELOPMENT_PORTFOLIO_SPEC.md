# OFFICIAL NDMU FACULTY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM PORTFOLIO FORMAT SPECIFICATION (`NDMU_FACULTY_DEVELOPMENT_PORTFOLIO_SPEC.md`)

> **Source**: `Faculty-Development-Portfolio-Dollaga.docx` (Notre Dame of Marbel University HR Template)  
> **Document Purpose**: Official formal portfolio submitted by academic personnel for annual Faculty Development Program (FDP) evaluation, ranking, promotion, and tenure assessment.

---

## 📄 Formal HR Document Page Sequence

The portfolio strictly adheres to the official HR document layout. **Decorative cover pages and index pages are omitted.**

1. **Page 1: Formal HR Header & Sections A–B**
   - Centered Formal Header: `FACULTY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM` / `Portfolio`
   - Metadata line 1: `Name: [Faculty Full Name]` | `Status: Full-Time - Permanent / Contractual`
   - Metadata line 2: `School Year: [2023-2024]` | `Rank: [Academic Rank]`
   - Section A: Professional Development (Education, Memberships, Seminars)
   - Section B: Productivity & Creative Work (Guest Lecturer / Consultant)
2. **Page 2: Formal HR Sections B–C & Signature Block**
   - Section B (Cont.): Publications, Conduct of Research, Awards, Materials, Creative Work
   - Section C: Service and Leadership (Extra-Curricular / Club Moderation, Community Involvement, Years of Service at NDMU)
   - Signature Block: `________________________________ Signature over Printed Name`
3. **Pages 3+: Attached Certificate Proof Documents**
   - Scanned PDF/Image certificates attached in **reverse chronological order** (most recent date first).

## 🎨 Visual Parity Rules

- **Typography**: Formal Serif font family (`font-serif`, Georgia / Times New Roman).
- **Section Headers**: Capitalized bold text with dark navy blue background (`#0f2537` / `#1b4332`) and white text.
- **Table Column Headers**: Light cyan/blue background (`#e6f2ff` / `#f0f7f4`) with italicized text (`Date(s)`, `Course/Degree`, `School/University`, `Remarks`).
