# PLAN 12 — Phase 4 Read-Only & Correction UX Contract
## AchieveNest — Student Profile & Institutional Relationship Visibility

---

# 1. Read-Only Governance & Correction Policy

1. **Explicit Read-Only Design**:
   - Institutional sections are marked with an explicit `Institutional Record (Read-Only)` badge.
   - Disabled edit icons (e.g. disabled gray pencil buttons) are completely omitted (`Disabled edit icons = 0`) to prevent user frustration.
2. **Authoritative Correction Workflow**:
   - The UI includes a prominent informational notice:
     > *"Academic placements, year levels, and institutional advisor assignments are officially maintained by the Office of Student Affairs & Services (OSAD) and the Office of the Registrar. If any details are incorrect, please contact the OSAD office for official record reconciliation."*
   - This prevents students from attempting unsupported client-side mutations.
