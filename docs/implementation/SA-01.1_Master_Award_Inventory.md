## 1. Document Purpose & Phase Objective

This document represents the authoritative deliverable for **SA-01.1: Master Award Inventory**.

The objective of this phase is to freeze a complete, stable, source-backed inventory of every award processed by the student award module before proceeding to criterion, sub-category, and scoring route mapping in downstream phases (SA-01.2 through SA-01.5).

Every award recorded in this inventory satisfies the following criteria:
1. **Stable Identifier:** Immutable award code and persistent UUID.
2. **Official Name:** Verified institutional nomenclature.
3. **Explicit Eligibility Gates:** Award-level eligibility constraints (graduating status, sex restriction) backed by authoritative sources.
4. **Calculated Score Ceilings:** Distinct identification of Full Rubric Maximum, Automatically Computable Maximum, and Manually Evaluated Maximum.
5. **80% Portfolio Potential Score Threshold:** Mathematically calculated strictly as $\text{Auto-Computable Max} \times 0.80$.
6. **Provenance & Fidelity Status:** Clear demarcation between active verified awards and quarantined legacy records.
