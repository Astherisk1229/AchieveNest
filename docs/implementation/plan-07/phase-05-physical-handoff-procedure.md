# Secure Physical Handoff Procedure

---

## 1. Step-by-Step Administrator Handoff Protocol

1. **Print Verification**: Review print preview to confirm only one credential slip is printed.
2. **Immediate Retrieval**: Collect the slip immediately from the printer output tray to prevent third-party exposure.
3. **Recipient Identity Verification**: Verify the physical recipient's identity against their Student ID, Personnel ID, or government-issued photo ID.
4. **Handoff & Guidance**:
   - Hand the slip directly to the account owner.
   - Explain that login requires their **Institutional Email** (`@ndmu.edu.ph`), not their Student/Personnel ID.
   - Inform them that the system mandates an immediate personal password change upon first sign-in.
   - Instruct them to shred or destroy the printed slip immediately after password activation.
5. **Compromise / Misdirection Handling**:
   - If the slip is lost, misdirected, or viewed by an unauthorized party, immediately invoke **Reset Temporary Password** in AchieveNest to issue a new credential and invalidate the old one.
