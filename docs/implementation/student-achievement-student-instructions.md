# AchieveNest — Student User Guide
## How to Submit Co-Curricular & Extra-Curricular Achievements

---

### Welcome to AchieveNest Student Portfolio!

AchieveNest allows you to maintain an authoritative, verified digital portfolio of your accomplishments, leadership roles, seminar participation, athletic achievements, and service activities during your academic journey at Notre Dame of Marbel University.

---

### Step-by-Step Guide to Adding an Achievement

#### 1. Start a New Record
- Navigate to the **Student Dashboard** or **Achievements** tab.
- Click the green **+ Add Achievement / Portfolio Record** button.

#### 2. Enter Basic Information
- **Title**: Enter the specific name of your accomplishment (e.g. *PRISAA Regional Basketball Meet*, *12th Regional Research Congress*).
- **Organizer / Issuing Body**: Enter the organization, department, or external body that hosted the event.
- **Start / End Dates**: Provide the accurate date(s) of the event.
- **Description**: Add brief contextual notes about your experience.

#### 3. Choose Classification
- **Primary Category**: Select the domain that best describes the activity (e.g. *Leadership Position*, *Sports*, *Seminar / Training*).
- **Subcategory**: Select the specific event type.

> **Helpful Tip on Categories:**
> - If you attended a **leadership seminar** or workshop, select **Seminar / Training** -> **Leadership Development**.
> - If you participated in a **sports clinic or skills training**, select **Seminar / Training** -> **Sports Development**.
> - If you competed in a **tournament**, select **Sports** -> (Your Sport). Enter your placement (Champion, Runner-Up) in the *Structured Details* section.

#### 4. Fill in the Structured Details
- When you pick a subcategory, specific details will appear (such as your Officer Level, Placement / Result, Event Level, or Volunteer Hours).
- Fill in each required field accurately.

#### 5. Upload Supporting Evidence
- Attach scanned copies or photos of your Certificate of Recognition, Certificate of Participation, Official Endorsement, or Event Photos.
- Supported file types: PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG (Max 10MB per file).

#### 6. Save Draft or Submit
- **Save Draft**: Saves your progress so you can return and finish it later.
- **Submit for Verification**: Sends the record to your Program Coordinator or OSAD for official verification.

---

### Tracking Your Submission Status

- **Draft**: Saved in your workspace; you can edit anytime.
- **Submitted**: In queue for Program Coordinator review.
- **Revisions Requested**: Your coordinator requested clarification or additional evidence. Read their remarks, edit the record, and click *Resubmit*.
- **Verified**: Officially verified and credited to your master student portfolio!
