# Personnel Evaluation Track — Plan F — Phase F5: Plan A Integration & Boundary Verification

## Integration Boundary: Plan A (OCR & Accomplishment Entry Guidance)

### Plan A Responsibilities:
- OCR evidence scanning;
- Text parsing and confidence scoring;
- Upload guidance and field suggestions;
- Advisory point estimation for personnel viewing.

### Verification of Plan F Isolation:
- **Zero Plan A Authority on Accepted Points**: Plan A suggestions remain strictly advisory. Official scoring payloads in Plan F originate exclusively from backend rules or evaluator judgment.
- **No Direct Determination**: Plan A never computes or persists `Passed` or `Retained`.
- **Evidence Requirement Compliance**: Plan F validates that evidence attachments match the criterion's required proof without reading or relying on Plan A's internal OCR confidence values.
